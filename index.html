Hermes

Hermes is a private, self-hosted assistant used by a single person (the owner of this page) to help with personal study and admin tasks. It is not a public product, is not offered to other users, and has no sign-up.

Privacy policy

Who uses it. Only the owner, signed in to their own Google account. No other Google users are authorised.

What Google data it accesses.

  • Gmail – read-only (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or modify email.
  • Google Drive – files inside one folder chosen by the owner, for reading and editing.
  • Google Sheets and Google Calendar – the owner's own spreadsheets and calendar.

How the data is used. Only to carry out tasks the owner asks for (for example, summarising the owner's own email or updating the owner's own files). Data is processed on a server controlled by the owner and by the AI model provider the owner has chosen, solely to perform those tasks.

Sharing. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to train general AI/ML models. It is not transferred to anyone except as needed to perform the owner's own requests.

Storage and security. OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's private server with restricted file permissions. Nothing is published.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Compliance. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
